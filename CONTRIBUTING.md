# Contributing

This repository is maintained by [Jinyuan Liu](https://github.com/jinyuanliu047). The maintainer controls edits and merges to `main`.

To suggest an improvement, open an issue or fork the repository and submit a pull request. Explain the writing problem, your proposed change, and a short public or synthetic example when useful. Changes to `main` require the maintainer’s review; outside contributors do not receive direct write access.

Do not submit confidential manuscripts, private data, credentials, or copyrighted article PDFs. Preserve research choices and distinguish evidence from possible explanations. Feedback should identify the passage and explain how the change improves its meaning or flow.
